Recipe ideas

Crispy tofu

Press, cut into cubes, toss in cornstarch and fry until golden. Great in bowls and wraps.

Tofu kebab

Marinate with ras el hanout, garlic and lemon, then grill on skewers.

Tofu is not feta

Fresh tofu is mild and neutral – it takes on the flavour of your marinade.
